FAQ

Good questions.

I already use a booking tool. Is switching a pain?

No. Send us your current services, prices, staff hours and policies and we set up your new page for you. Keep your old tool running until you're happy, then point your links to Booked Solid.

How do deposits and payments work?

Deposits are paid by card through Stripe when the client books, and go to your own Stripe account. You choose the amount per service, staff member or first visit.

Who owns my client list?

You do. Your client and booking information is yours, is used only to run your bookings, and you can ask for a full export at any time.

Is there a contract?

No long contract. It's one flat monthly price and you can cancel anytime.

How long does setup take?

Usually a day once we have your services and rules. You review the page before it goes live.

Can each person on my team have their own schedule?

Yes. Every staff member gets their own hours, services and session lengths, and clients can choose who they book with.
