Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated November 25, 2026. Booked Solid is operated by MUPPBURY HOLDINGS LLC.

Cancel your subscription

Cancel any time by emailing eatsaiofficial@gmail.com. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period and you are not charged again. There are no contracts or cancellation fees.

Subscription refunds

If you were charged by mistake, or you cancel within 7 days of your first payment, email us and we will refund that payment in full. After that, fees are not refunded for partial months.

Client deposits

A deposit is a payment to the shop you booked with, not to Booked Solid. Each shop sets its own cancellation policy, shown when you book. Refunds are decided and issued by the shop. When a deposit is fully refunded, the 1% client booking fee (minimum $0.50) is refunded with it. Refunds return to the original card, usually within 5 to 10 business days.
